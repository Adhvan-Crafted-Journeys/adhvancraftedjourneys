Day-by-Day Itinerary

DAY 01

Arrival in Dubai

  • Meet & greet at DXB airport exit.
  • Transfer by 50-seater AC deluxe coach with tour guide.
  • Standard hotel check-in at 3:00 PM.
  • Evening Dhow Cruise Dinner at Marina.
  • 2-hour sailing.
  • International buffet dinner.
  • Upper deck seating.
  • Tanoura dance.
  • Return to hotel.

DAY 02

Dubai City Tour

  • Breakfast.
  • Transfer by 50-seater AC deluxe coach with tour guide.
  • Dubai city tour.
  • Photo stop at Dubai Frame.
  • Drive through Palm Jumeirah.
  • Photo stop at Jumeirah Public Beach.
  • Photo stop at Museum of the Future.
  • Proceed to Dubai Mall.
  • Return to hotel.

DAY 03

Burj Khalifa

  • Breakfast.
  • One-way transfer from hotel to Dubai World Trade Centre.
  • Evening transfer with tour guide.
  • Burj Khalifa — Level 124/125.
  • Non-peak entrance ticket.
  • Return to hotel.

DAY 04

Global Village

  • Breakfast.
  • One-way transfer from hotel to Dubai World Trade Centre.
  • Evening transfer with tour guide.
  • Global Village entrance ticket.
  • Return to hotel.

DAY 05

Dolphin Show / Museum of the Future

  • Breakfast.
  • One-way transfer from hotel to Dubai World Trade Centre.
  • Evening transfer with tour guide.
  • Dolphin Show OR Museum of the Future.
  • Supplement cost applies.
  • Subject to pre-booking 50 days prior to arrival.
  • Return to hotel.

DAY 06

Leisure / Abu Dhabi Tour

  • Breakfast.
  • Free day for leisure OR Abu Dhabi tour with supplement cost.
  • Transfer with tour guide.
  • BAPS Temple.
  • Drive through Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque.
  • Ferrari World entrance.
  • Drive through Abu Dhabi Corniche.
  • Return to hotel.

DAY 07

Miracle Garden & Desert Safari

  • Breakfast.
  • Transfer by 50-seater AC deluxe coach with guide.
  • Miracle Garden entrance.
  • Desert Safari experience.
  • 6 persons per SUV.

Desert Safari activities

  • Dune bash
  • Sunset stop
  • Camel ride
  • Sand board
  • BBQ dinner
  • Arabic coffee with dates at camp entrance
  • Belly dance
  • Tanoura dance
  • Fire show
  • Return to hotel.

DAY 08

Shopping & Departure

  • Breakfast.
  • Checkout.
  • Transfer by 50-seater AC deluxe coach with guide.
  • Shopping at Gold Souq / Meena Bazar.
  • Transfer to DXB airport for departure.
