A festival rooted in gratitude

More than a festival.
A way of life.

Pongal is Tamil Nadu's cherished harvest festival — a time to give thanks to the Sun, the land, cattle, farmers and the people who make each harvest possible.

Step beyond the usual sightseeing and experience the season through village Pongal traditions, historic irrigation heritage, Thanjavur craftsmanship and archaeological discoveries.
